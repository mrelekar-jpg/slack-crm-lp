Frequently asked questions

How long is the free trial?
Most Salesforce free trials last 14–30 days depending on the product. Slack CRM is included free with Slack Business+.
What features are included?
Trials include full access to the product's features — sales automation, lead management, contact management, reporting, and more.
Do I need a credit card?
No credit card is required for most free trials. You can start exploring immediately.
What happens after the trial ends?
You can choose a plan that fits your needs, or your data will be saved for a limited time if you decide to come back later.
Can I upgrade from Slack CRM to Salesforce Starter?
Yes. Slack CRM is built on Salesforce, so your data carries over seamlessly — no migration needed. Just upgrade when you're ready for more.
